01 · The essentials

Fat is not the enemy.

Dietary fat is an essential nutrient. The type of fat and the foods it comes from matter.

⚡

Energy

Fat provides 9 kilocalories per gram, a concentrated source of energy.

🧬

Cell function

Fats are important components of cell membranes.

🌞

Vitamin absorption

Dietary fat helps absorb vitamins A, D, E and K.

🛡️

Protection

Body fat helps insulate the body and cushion organs.

02 · Know the categories

Four families of dietary fats.

Foods often contain a mix of fatty acids, but one type may be more prominent.

🧈

Saturated

Found in butter, cheese, fatty meats and some tropical oils. Replace some with unsaturated fats when possible.

LIMIT
🫒

Monounsaturated

Found in olive oil, avocado and many nuts. A useful choice in a balanced eating pattern.

CHOOSE MORE OFTEN
🐟

Polyunsaturated

Found in fatty fish, walnuts and flaxseed. Includes omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids.

CHOOSE MORE OFTEN
🍟

Trans fats

Industrial trans fats can raise LDL and lower HDL cholesterol. Avoid partially hydrogenated oils.

AVOID INDUSTRIAL TRANS FAT
03 · Everyday choices

Healthy swaps, made simple.

Think about what you can replace—not simply what you can remove.

✓ Choose more often

  • Unsaturated plant oils
  • Avocado
  • Nuts and seeds
  • Fish, including oily fish

◷ Limit / avoid

  • Butter and fatty meats
  • Foods high in saturated fat
  • Industrial trans fats
  • Partially hydrogenated oils
04 · In the body

Why fat quality matters.

❤️

Heart health

Replacing saturated fats with unsaturated fats can improve blood cholesterol patterns.

🧠

Brain function

Fatty acids are part of brain-cell membranes and support normal nervous-system function.

🌱

Whole-body health

Dietary fats support cell membranes, vitamin absorption and essential fatty-acid needs.
